Maker Trees

A maker tree hidden in the woodland.

A navigation system for indigenous people that exist long before GPS, paper maps, or metal trail markers.

Created by bending the flexible saplings parallel to the ground when they are young.

Overtime, the tree will adapted and locking in this permanent shape.

A man-made decoration made by arrangement of small branches.

These trees served a critical survival purpose.

Traditional and prescribed burn

Fire-dependant ecosystems, such as the rare black oak savannah, grow more vigorously in response to fire.

So the indigenous people use this carefully managed fire to stimulate the growth of this ecosystems.

How ever the collonizer suppersess suppressed Indigenous burning.

Luckily, this burn is back and is called "traditional and prescribed burn" to honor the indigenous people
